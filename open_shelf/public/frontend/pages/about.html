About Open Shelf

Open Shelf Reading Foundation is built around the idea that access to books, knowledge and welcoming spaces can help people learn, discover new ideas and build meaningful connections.

Our space combines a bookstore and library experience with reading, working and community-oriented activities. Members and visitors can explore books, use our space and participate in programmes designed to encourage learning and creativity.
